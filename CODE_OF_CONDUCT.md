# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical perspective.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss technical ideas on their merits.
- Welcome questions, corrections, and different experiences.
- Focus feedback on code, documentation, and decisions rather than people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussion or contribution work.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer at lucasmafrapinheiro@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
